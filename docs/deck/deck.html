Why this project

Fuel dealers run a seasonal business with two peaks

Winter

heating oil · propane

Fuel delivery volume spikes with cold weather. The Northeast is the heating oil capital of the U.S., and propane is common in rural areas.

Summer

AC install · repair

Fuel slows down, but full-service dealers keep crews busy installing and repairing air conditioning.

The gap

no public dataset

Nobody publishes monthly fuel demand by county. Software vendors serving these dealers have to guess where the demand is.

Goal: build that view from public data, so dispatch, routing and billing platforms can see where demand is, how it moves through the year, and which dealers fit best.

Demand model

Homes × usage, shaped by the weather

est_gallons(county, fuel, month) =
  homes(county, fuel)
  × [ gal_per_hdd(state, fuel) × HDD(county, month)
    + base_gal(state, fuel) / 12 ]
HomesOccupied homes heating with oil or propane, from Census ACS.
UsageRECS heating gallons per home, divided by 2020 degree days, plus a steady base.
WeatherEach county's own NOAA degree days, so cold winters burn more.
ChecksPriced with EIA SHOPP, then calibrated against EIA SEDS state totals.
